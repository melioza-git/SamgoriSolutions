Privacy

Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it. Last updated October 20, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Samgori Solutions, a consultancy in the New York Metro Area. You can reach us at maia.eliozashvili@samgorisolutions.com.

What we collect

Information you send us. If you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, and message, and optionally your company, NetSuite release and the number of custom scripts. We use them only to reply to you and to discuss possible work. We do not sell them.

Website usage data. We use Google Analytics, through a Google tag, to understand how visitors use the site. Google sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google servers when a page loads and Google receives your IP address.

How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and arrange consultations.
  • To see which pages are useful and improve the site.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that make the site work: Google (analytics and fonts), our website host, and the email service that delivers contact form messages. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing the address above.

Retention

We keep contact form messages for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting work, and delete them on request. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Google account.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
